# Formatting, Voice & Profile

## Visual rules

- **One idea per line. One line per paragraph.** A 200-word post typically has 20–25 line breaks. It should read like a column of headlines.
- **Blank line between every line.** White space is what makes it readable on mobile.
- **First 2 lines carry the post.** Everything after ~140–210 characters is hidden behind "…see more".
- **Length:** 150–250 words for standard posts. Under 1,300 characters keeps the whole thing near-scannable. Long-form (2,000+) works only when the story genuinely earns it.
- **Sub-points:** use `→` or `>` for indentation. LinkedIn strips markdown — no `-`, `*`, `#`, or `**bold**` syntax.
- **Bold:** roughly one phrase every 3–4 lines, on the sentences a skimmer must catch. Requires a Unicode text formatter; tell the user rather than emitting markdown.
- **Emojis:** 0–3, functional not decorative. Never in the hook line.
- **Hashtags:** 0–3, at the very end, broad + niche.
- **Links:** never in the body. First comment, then edit the post to say "link in comments".
- **Numbers:** digits, not words. "7 things", not "seven things".
- **@mentions:** only when the person will plausibly engage — a dead mention hurts distribution.

## Voice calibration

Ask for 2–3 of the user's past posts before writing in their voice. Mirror:
- Average sentence length
- Punctuation habits (dashes? ellipses? all-lowercase?)
- Vocabulary and any recurring phrases
- How direct their opinions are
- Whether they swear, joke, use slang

Then write a one-line voice note back to them: "Sounds like: short sentences, dry humour, no emojis, opinions stated flatly." Confirm before scaling to a batch.

**Do not** import Lara's own register ("babe", "queen", "literally", "slay") unless it's authentically the user's. Borrowed voice reads as cosplay.

## De-AI checklist

Strip every one of these:

- "In today's fast-paced world" / "In an era of"
- "Let's dive in" / "Let's unpack this" / "Buckle up"
- "It's not just X, it's Y" (unless genuinely earned once)
- "Game-changer", "leverage", "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless", "robust", "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "realm"
- "I'm thrilled/humbled/excited to announce"
- Em-dash-heavy rhythm; three-item lists in every sentence
- Perfectly parallel structure across every bullet
- Summary paragraphs restating what was just said
- Hedging: "arguably", "it could be said", "in many ways"
- Generic closers: "What are your thoughts?" / "Would love to hear your perspective"
- Rhetorical question stacks

Replace with: concrete nouns, specific numbers, one clear opinion, an uneven rhythm, and a question only *your* audience could answer.

## Grammar and rhythm

- Fragments are fine. Start sentences with And, But, So.
- Active voice. Present tense for stories where it adds immediacy.
- Vary line length deliberately — three short lines then one longer one creates momentum.
- Second person ("you") for teaching sections; first person for story sections.

## Profile optimisation

The post drives the profile visit. The profile has to confirm the promise.

**Headline (220 chars).** Not your job title. Formula:
`[What you do] for [specific market] | [proof or mechanism] | [offer/CTA]`
Example shape: "I help B2B founders turn LinkedIn into pipeline | 40+ brands, $2M+ sourced | Free playbook below"

**Banner.** One sentence stating who you help and how. Treat it as a billboard, not decoration.

**Profile photo.** Face fills the frame, clear background, consistent across platforms.

**About section.** Write it like a post:
1. Hook line (visible before "see more")
2. The problem your market has, in their words
3. Your credibility — specific numbers, names, outcomes
4. What you do about it (the mechanism, named)
5. Who it's for and who it isn't for
6. One clear CTA (link, DM keyword, lead magnet)

**Featured section.** Lead magnet first, best-performing post second, proof/case study third.

**Custom URL.** Set it. `linkedin.com/in/firstnamelastname`.

**Experience.** Rewrite the current role as outcomes, not duties.

## Metrics that matter

Track these, in order:
1. **Profile views** — the only intent signal that precedes a DM.
2. **Comments (and comment depth)** — long discussions extend distribution.
3. **Followers from ICP**, not raw follower count.
4. **Inbound DMs / lead magnet opt-ins.**
5. Impressions — the noisiest metric. Watch the trend, not the spikes.

Review monthly: pull your top 5 posts, identify which template and hook formula each used, and run those structures more often.
