# Post Templates

12 fill-in structures. Pick by intent, never at random. Replace every `[bracket]` — if a value is unknown, ask the user rather than inventing it.

**Contents:** 1 Classic SLAY · 2 Expectation-break · 3 Specificity stack · 4 Counter-intuitive reframe · 5 Client-outcome case study · 6 Mistake/failure · 7 Listicle-with-a-spine · 8 Before/after · 9 Myth-bust · 10 EduSales · 11 Halo effect · 12 Carousel outline

---

## 1. Classic SLAY (default)

```
[One line: a moment with tension. Concrete, specific, surprising.]

[Line of context.]
[Line of stakes — why it mattered.]
[The turn — what changed.]

The lesson:

[One sentence. The principle the story earned.]

Here's how to apply it:

1. [Action]
2. [Action]
3. [Action]
4. [Action]

[Closing line that summarises the post in one sentence.]

[Question answerable in one sentence.]
```

*Worked example (opener):* "Last week a client told me my pricing was insulting. I doubled it the next day." → Lesson: "When someone calls your price insulting, they're telling you you're priced for the wrong customer."

## 2. Expectation break

```
[Statement that violates a professional norm. Stated flatly, no hedging.]

Everyone told me [conventional expectation].

Here's what actually happened:

→ [Result 1]
→ [Result 2]
→ [Result 3]

The reason it worked:

[One sentence.]

[3 steps to replicate]

[Question.]
```

---

## 3. Specificity stack (authority post)

```
In [timeframe], I went from [start state] to [end state].

Here's what nobody told me:

[3 lines of story, including the inflection moment.]

The 5 things I did differently:

1. [Tactic] — [why it worked, 6 words]
2. [Tactic] — [why]
3. [Tactic] — [why]
4. [Tactic] — [why]
5. [Tactic] — [why]

[Summary line.]

[Question or CTA.]
```

## 4. Counter-intuitive reframe

```
[Advice everyone repeats.]

I disagree.

[2–3 line story that earned the disagreement.]

The reframe:

[One-sentence inversion.]

3 ways to do it instead:

1. [Action]
2. [Action]
3. [Action]

[Question aimed at your ICP's daily reality.]
```

## 5. Client-outcome case study

```
[Client type] came to me [timeframe] ago with [specific problem].

[Their situation in 2 lines — numbers if possible.]

Here's what we changed:

→ [Change 1]
→ [Change 2]
→ [Change 3]

Result: [specific measurable outcome].

The part most people skip:

[One sentence — the non-obvious lever.]

[Question.]
```

## 6. Mistake / failure post

```
I lost [specific thing] because of [specific decision].

[2 lines: what you believed at the time.]

[1 line: what it actually cost.]

What I'd do differently:

1. [Correction]
2. [Correction]
3. [Correction]

[One-sentence lesson.]

[Question inviting others' version of the mistake.]
```

## 7. Listicle with a spine

Never a bare list. Frame → list → lesson.

```
[Problem statement your ICP feels weekly.]

I've [credential: audited/built/shipped X].

The [N] things that separate [good] from [bad]:

1. [Item]
   → [One line of why]
2. [Item]
   → [Why]
3. [Item]
   → [Why]

Most people get [item N] wrong because [reason].

[Question.]
```

---

## 8. Before / after

```
Two versions of the same [thing].

Before:
[Quote or short paste of weak version]

After:
[Strong version]

Difference: [one sentence].

Why it matters: [one sentence tied to their outcome].

[Question: which version does yours look like?]
```

## 9. Myth-bust

```
"[Myth as a quote.]"

This is costing [audience] [specific cost].

Here's the truth:

[2–3 lines with evidence — numbers, examples, your own data.]

What to do instead:

1. [Action]
2. [Action]
3. [Action]

[Question.]
```

## 10. EduSales post (teach, then permission-to-sell)

```
[Outcome the reader wants], in [timeframe].

Here's the exact process:

1. [Step]
2. [Step]
3. [Step]
4. [Step]

[Proof line: a result this produced — yours or a client's.]

[One-sentence lesson.]

I put the full [template / checklist / breakdown] together.

Comment "[keyword]" and I'll send it over.
```

Rule: teach enough that it works standalone. The offer is depth and implementation, not withheld information.

---

## 11. Halo-effect post

```
[Specific moment: stage, room, collab, milestone.]

[2 lines of honest context — what it took, what surprised you.]

[1 line of humility or a lesson about the people involved.]

What I took away:

→ [Takeaway]
→ [Takeaway]

[Question or thank-you that names people.]
```

Small wins count: first client, first talk, first inbound DM.

---

## 12. Carousel / document outline

```
Slide 1 (cover):  [Hook — 6–9 words, biggest text on the slide]
Slide 2:          [The problem, one sentence]
Slide 3–8:        [One idea per slide. Headline + max 2 lines.]
Slide 9:          [Recap: the 3 things that matter]
Slide 10 (CTA):   [Follow / comment keyword / save this]

Caption: full SLAY post in miniature — hook, 2-line context,
"swipe →", one-sentence lesson, question.
```

Keep consistent fonts, colours and layout across every carousel — visual signature drives brand recall before the name is read.
