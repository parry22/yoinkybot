# Distribution & Conversion

Content creates attention. Distribution makes sure it's seen. Conversion turns it into clients. Skipping any layer breaks the system — most people only do the first.

## The weekly operating rhythm

**Weekly (3–5 posts)**
- 1 authority/positioning post (your mechanism, your opinion)
- 1 story post (SLAY)
- 1 tactical/teaching post
- 1 media or format-different post (video, carousel, image)
- 1 celebration/proof post (client win, milestone, testimonial)

Tuesday and Wednesday tend to perform best. Solo operators with no repurposing system should start at 3/week and only scale when the system exists.

**Monthly**
- Halo-effect content: events, collabs, podcasts, being in rooms with credible people. Borrowed trust is real trust at the start.
- Content review: top 5 posts → which template, which hook formula, which pillar. Do more of what won.
- One lead magnet refresh or new one.

## Idea generation (never stare at a blank page)

Pull from these five wells, in order:
1. **Problems you solved for yourself** — the most defensible content you own.
2. **Problems you solved for clients** — anonymise, keep the numbers.
3. **Questions you get asked repeatedly** — in DMs, on calls, in comments.
4. **Something on your feed you strongly agree or disagree with** — a reaction post with your own evidence.
5. **Your own top-performing posts** — reuse the framework with new content.

Batch: pick one idea, write it as a text post, then repurpose the same idea into a carousel, a video script, and a newsletter section. One idea, four assets.

## Repurposing ladder

```
Core idea
├── Text post (SLAY)
├── Carousel (10 slides, one idea per slide)
├── 60–90s video (hook in 3 seconds, one lesson, CTA)
├── Newsletter section
└── Lead magnet chapter
```

Video is not dead — it proves you're real and that you know the topic. As AI content increases, proof-of-human raises trust.

## Conversion: EduSales (light)

Teach so well that a stranger gets a result from a free post. Sell the implementation.

**The funnel**
1. **Attention** — posts, video
2. **Lead magnet** — the bridge from attention to a contactable person
3. **Email list** — where you build depth
4. **Offer** — landing page, masterclass, or call
5. **Ascension** — upsells, retainers, product ladder

**Lead magnet patterns that convert**
- The swipe file ("47 hooks I've used")
- The checklist/audit ("the 12-point profile audit")
- The template ("the exact 5 posts I write every week")
- The teardown ("I rewrote 10 real posts — here's each before/after")
- The mini-course (3 emails, one result)

Deliver via a link in the first comment when needed. No links in the post body.

## Post-publish checklist

- [ ] Link in first comment (if any)
- [ ] First comment adds context, not just a link
- [ ] Logged the post's template + hook formula for monthly review

## What no longer works

- Engagement pods and reciprocal-like groups
- Posting and ghosting
- Format-chasing (carousels are not inherently better than text)
- Generic viral bait and unattached personal stories
- Topic-hopping — the algorithm and your audience both need topic consistency
- Volume without positioning: 5 vague posts lose to 2 specific ones
