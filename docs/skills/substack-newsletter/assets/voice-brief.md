# Voice & Positioning Brief

Fill this out once. Reuse it as context for every draft afterward. Answer in specifics — vague answers here produce vague newsletters.

---

## 1. The reader

**Who, precisely?** A person in a situation, not a demographic.
> e.g. "A solo consultant in year two who is billing well but has no inbound and is starting to panic about it."

**What do they already know?** (Determines how much you explain.)

**What do they want that they can't easily get?**

**What do they secretly worry about?** (This is where the best hooks live.)

**Where do they already hang out online?**

## 2. The promise

**One sentence. Every issue delivers ______.**

Test it: could a reader describe this to a friend in one sentence, and would that friend either subscribe or clearly not? If both would shrug, it's too broad.

**What this newsletter is NOT about.** (Naming the exclusions is what makes the promise legible.)
