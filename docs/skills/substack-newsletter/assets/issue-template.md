## Body

### Hook
*1–3 sentences. Scene, number, contradiction, private question, or confession. Nothing that only announces what you're about to say.*

### The turn
*2–4 sentences. Name the tension. Why is the obvious answer wrong or incomplete? This is what makes them finish.*
