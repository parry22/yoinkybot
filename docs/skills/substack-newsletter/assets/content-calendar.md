# Content Calendar

Planning removes the weekly panic. The point isn't rigidity — it's that you never start from a blank page.

---

## The idea bank

Keep one running list, always. Capture anything that makes you think "huh." Every idea gets a pillar tag and a rough format.

| Idea | Pillar | Format | Source | Ready? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | reader question / own experience / news / research | |

**Where ideas come from, in descending order of quality:**
1. Reader questions and replies — pre-validated demand
2. Questions you get asked repeatedly in real life
3. Things you had to figure out the hard way
4. Something you disagree with that's currently being said in your space
5. A pattern you noticed across several unrelated things
6. Something someone else covered badly

If the bank ever drops below 10 items, spend a session refilling it rather than writing.

**Build in buffer.** One flex week per quarter absorbs illness, travel, and life. Publications die from missed sends more than from bad posts.

**Bank two evergreen pieces.** Written, edited, ready to ship. Insurance against a bad week.

## Weekly rhythm

A sustainable version, adapt the days:

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| Mon | Pick the topic from the bank; outline; gather sources |
| Tue | Draft — no editing while drafting |
| Wed | Structural pass, then line pass |
| Thu | Machine-tell pass; subject lines; packaging; send |
| Fri | Atomize into Notes; engage with other writers; refill the idea bank |

Drafting and editing on different days is the highest-leverage scheduling decision available. Distance makes you a better editor of your own work.

---

## Monthly

- One collaboration outreach — guest swap, interview, cross-promo, or joint session
- Review metrics: CTOR, replies, growth by source, unsubscribes
- Note which posts overperformed and why. Do more of that.
- Check the welcome sequence still reflects reality

## Quarterly

- Refresh the About page and homepage
- Prune subscribers inactive for 6+ months
- Review positioning against `assets/voice-brief.md` — has the promise drifted?
- Update or replace the lead magnet
- Decide one thing to stop doing
